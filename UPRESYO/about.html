About UPRESYO

Know the price before you go.

UPRESYO lists canteen meals and ride fares around UP Mindanao in one place. Check what Castillo, Kalimudan and Ate Lings are serving, see how much a jeepney or tricycle ride costs, and plan your budget before you leave the room.

We built it for UP Mindanao students who want to know if they have enough for lunch and the ride home.
